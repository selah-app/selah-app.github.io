Selah — Support

Something not working, or a question the app doesn’t answer? Start here, and if this page doesn’t cover it, write to us — a person reads every message.

Get in touch: open Settings → Feedback in the app, which lets you attach screenshots, or email n.grantmcgregor@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

I changed phones and my history is missing

Sign in with the same account you used before — Apple, Google, or the same email address — and your sessions and reading progress come back down from the server. If you signed up with Apple and then signed in with Google, those are two separate accounts, which is the usual cause.

A contact isn’t showing up

Matching by phone only works when both people have added a phone number to their own profile, and when the numbers are stored in the same form. If it still doesn’t find them, the surest route is a QR code: have them open their profile, and scan it from the contacts screen.

I’m not getting notifications

Check two places. In your device’s Settings, confirm notifications are allowed for Selah. In the app, check that the specific notifications you want are switched on — reminders and companion alerts are controlled separately.

I don’t want someone seeing my progress

Visibility is set per person, not globally. Open that contact and choose what they can see; you can also remove the connection entirely, which stops all sharing immediately.

The widget or Lock Screen isn’t updating

Open the app once — the widget refreshes from the app rather than on its own, and iOS also limits how often it may redraw. If it’s still stale after that, remove the widget and add it again.

I want to delete my account

Settings → Account → Delete. This removes your profile, practice history, reading schedules, and connections from our servers straight away. It cannot be undone, so export or note anything you want to keep first.

Reporting a bug

Settings → Feedback, and please attach a screenshot if you can. Knowing what you tapped just before it went wrong is usually what makes a bug findable.
