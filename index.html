Privacy Policy for Selah

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Selah (“the app”, “we”) is a devotional practice app: a session timer, Bible reading schedules, and optional connections with people you practice alongside. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it’s used, and what control you have over it.

Data we collect

Account information. If you create an account (via Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email), we store your email address, a username, and an optional display name, so you can be identified to contacts you connect with.

Basic profile information. During setup the app asks for your gender and, in some cases, your birth year. This is stored privately and is never visible to other users; it is used only to determine who may connect with you.

Phone number and contacts. If you choose to find contacts from your phone’s contact list, the app reads your contact list on the device to extract phone numbers. Only normalized phone numbers — not names or any other contact details — are sent to our server to check for matches against other Selah users who have registered the same number. Your contact list is never uploaded or stored on our servers. Providing a phone number is optional.

Practice history. Your completed sessions (date, length, and duration) and your Bible reading progress are stored on our servers so that your history follows your account to a new or reinstalled device. Streaks, daily completion counts, and weekly averages are derived from this and shared with contacts only according to your own visibility settings, which you control per contact.

Reading schedules. Bible reading schedules you create, and any schedules you share view-only with another user, are stored on our servers so the people you share with can see them.

Notifications. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token issued by Apple or Google so we can deliver reminders and alerts about contact requests and companions’ activity. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

Approximate location. The first time you open the app while signed in, our server derives an approximate city, region, and country from your connection’s IP address, and stores only that city/region/country against your account for aggregate statistics about where the app is used. The IP address itself is used only for that lookup and is never stored or logged. This is recorded once per account, not continuously, and the app does not request or use your device’s GPS location.

Feedback you send. If you send feedback from Settings, the message, the category you chose, any screenshots you attach, and your account identity are emailed to the developer so the report can be answered.

Camera. The app uses the camera only to scan a contact’s QR code when you choose to add a contact that way. No images from the camera are stored or transmitted.

On-device data. Your session content, preferences, and a local copy of your history are stored on your device. Bible text you have read is cached on your device for offline use.

Data we don’t collect

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to operate the app’s features: authenticating you, keeping your practice history available across your devices, matching you with contacts you choose to connect with, showing practice progress to contacts per your settings, delivering notifications you have enabled, answering feedback you send, and counting users by city in aggregate. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts our backend (authentication, database, and server functions). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Apple and Google provide optional sign-in. If you use these, they handle your credentials directly — we only receive the name and email you authorize.
  • Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Expo deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Resend delivers transactional email, including feedback you send and contact-request notifications if you have chosen email delivery.
  • ipwho.is resolves an IP address to a city for the approximate-location lookup described above. The IP is sent for that request only and is not retained by us.

Your controls

  • You can change what practice data is visible to contacts at any time in Settings, per contact.
  • You can remove a contact connection, or stop sharing a reading schedule, at any time.
  • You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.
  • You can decline or revoke the contacts and camera permissions in your device settings; the app remains usable without them.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete). This immediately and irreversibly deletes your profile, practice history, reading schedules, contact connections, and shared progress data from our servers.

Data retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account, at which point it is deleted. Data stored on your device remains there until you delete the app or clear its data. Feedback emails you have sent are retained in the developer’s inbox so they can be answered.

Children’s privacy

Selah is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
